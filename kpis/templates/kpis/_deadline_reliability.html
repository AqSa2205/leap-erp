{% comment %} ============================================================ Deadline reliability — who hits their dates, per person and per milestone. Anchored on the DEADLINE falling in the period, not on when the work was done, so finished and unfinished work stay comparable: "of the milestones due this quarter, how many were met". Two things are deliberately kept apart. A completed milestone scores against the person who completed it. A milestone past its deadline that nobody has done is late but has NO actor — charging it to the previous stage's actor would blame the wrong person — so it shows per milestone instead, revealing where work is stuck even when the system cannot say who is holding it. ============================================================ {% endcomment %}
