"RFQs received" therefore agrees with the Sales "New opportunities" tile by construction — same event, two audiences — and a test pins them together so a silent redefinition of either is caught. "Submitted" is the INTERNAL handoff: the proposal team marking a BOM ready and sending it to sales for costing (CostingSheet.handed_over_at). It is NOT the client-facing technical proposal. Pending means the RFQ is still at the BOM stage — once the BOM is across it has left the proposal team's queue, whether or not sales has picked it up. Overdue is measured against the CLIENT submission deadline less two working days (KSA Fri/Sat weekend excluded, matching working_days_between). Sales cannot cost and submit in no time, so a BOM arriving inside that window is already late even though the client deadline has not passed. Received and submitted are period figures. Overdue and pending are snapshots of the queue right now, and are mutually exclusive: between them they cover every open RFQ whose BOM has not gone to sales. “BOMs sent to sales” is the internal handoff from the proposal desk, not the client proposal. Overdue counts BOMs not yet sent by two working days before the client submission deadline — sales needs that time to cost and submit. Received and sent cover the selected period; overdue and pending show the BOM queue now and do not change with it, and together account for every open RFQ whose BOM has not reached sales.
