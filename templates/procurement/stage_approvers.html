{% comment %} The distinction that stops this page being misread as a permission screen. Naming somebody here does not let them sign anything. {% endcomment %}
This decides who is told, not who is allowed. Permission to sign a stage still comes from the person's role, so naming somebody here who cannot sign would email them an invitation to press a button that refuses them.
Where a stage has nobody set, everyone holding that stage's role is emailed instead — noisier, but a missed approval costs more than a redundant email.
