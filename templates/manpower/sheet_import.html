Import Instructions

Your Excel file should have columns matching this structure:

Sr. No. Employees Name Department Designation DOJ Gross Salary Iqama Cost GOSI Cost ...
1 Ahmed Ali Engineering Lead Engineer 2023-01-15 204,000 2,500 18,900 ...

Recognized columns:

  • Employee info: Employees Name, Department, Classification of Staff, Location/Project, Designation, DOJ, Demobilization Date, Date of Birth
  • Costing: Budget Cost, Gross Salary, Iqama Cost, Services Transfer & Visa Fee, GOSI Cost, Vacation Pay, EXE Cost, EOSB, Air Ticket, Insurance Cost, Project Allowance, PPE, Engineering Council Cost, Other Expenditures
  • The header row is auto-detected within the first 5 rows
  • Total Yearly Cost, Monthly Cost, Daily Cost, and Hourly Cost are calculated automatically
  • The sheet title is taken from the Excel worksheet name
  • Supported formats: .xlsx, .xls
